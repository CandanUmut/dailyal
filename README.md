# Fahm (working name): hear the Quran and understand it

A phone-first daily program for understanding the Quran when you hear it. It teaches the most frequent words first, trains the ear with real recitation, and adds the small grammar that unlocks the most meaning. Its structure follows the validation app (Uu): spaced review, research first, offline.

**Status: Phase 0 (design proposal).** There is no app yet. Start here:

- `docs/design/DESIGN.md`: the design brief and the open decisions
- `docs/design/mockup/`: the phone mockup (`mockup.png`, one PNG per screen)
- `docs/research/sources.md`: data sources and their licences
- `docs/research/frequency.md`: how far the most frequent words take you
- `docs/CONTENT_PLAN.md`, `docs/CONTENT_GAPS.md`, `docs/PROGRESS.md`
